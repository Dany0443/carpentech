Meet the people behind it
How our robot works

Java code controls the motors that move our robot. A dedicated, controller-operated launcher propels balls toward the hoop, while a camera uses Java code to detect ball colors.

Building, programming, and testing happen together: a mechanical change gives us something new to tune in the code.

From Cărpineni,
with curiosity.

Our workshop starts at Liceul Teoretic Ștefan Holban. Here, we learn by designing, building, programming, and trying again.

Some of us work with mechanisms. Some write code. Others help our ideas reach people. The robot brings it all together.

We build.

We design and build our robot, then keep improving it until its parts work together.

We code.

We write Java, work on autonomous routines and driver assistance, and test how our software behaves on the robot.

We share.

We bring robotics to schools and community events, helping students try building and programming for themselves.
